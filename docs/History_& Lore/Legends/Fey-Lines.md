[[Fey-Lines|Fey-Lines]] are loosely connected streams of magical energy believed to flow beneath and through [[../A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]].

They are not visible like ordinary rivers, but their effects can sometimes be recognized in unusually vigorous life, strange magical behavior, old ruins, or materials formed near powerful convergences.

## Convergences

Where several lines meet, magical energy becomes significantly stronger.

These convergences shaped the development of older civilizations.

### Elven Use

Elven traditions used fey-line power to:

- Encourage extraordinary plant growth
- Sustain hidden woodland domains
- Conceal settlements
- Strengthen living landscapes

### Dwarven Use

The [[../../Factions_& Clans/Dwarven Clans/Dwarven_Clans|Dwarven Clans]] established many [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Delves/Delves|Delves]] near known convergences.

They used the energy to support deep construction, magical craft, and the extraction of rare materials such as [[./Fey-stone|Fey-stone]].

## Modern Understanding

Modern scholars can recognize some fey-line effects but do not possess a complete map of the network.

Old routes may weaken, shift, or behave differently after magical disasters.

Because fey-lines connect distant places, damage at one convergence can sometimes produce effects far from the obvious source.
