The [[Golden_Grouper|Golden Grouper]] is one of the better-known sailor stories of [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/Waters/Bishop's_Bay|Bishop's Bay]].

Legend describes an immense grouper, sometimes said to be as large as a whale, whose scales or flesh shine like worked gold.

## Origin of the Story

The most popular version claims the creature began as an ordinary goldfish belonging to a mad alchemist.

Something went wrong—or exactly right—with an experiment involving growth and transmutation.

The fish escaped, continued growing, and eventually became the monster of the modern tale.

## Sightings

No two descriptions agree on its exact size.

Sailors have blamed the Grouper for:

- Missing nets
- Damaged small boats
- Enormous shadows below the water
- Schools of fish suddenly fleeing an area
- Gold-colored flashes far below the surface

[[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/NPCs/Mirken_Stanton|Mirken Stanton]] is famously obsessed with finding it and insists the creature is real.
