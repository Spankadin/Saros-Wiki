The [[Storm_Knights|Storm Knights]] were one of the great human powers of the [[../Ages_of_Saros|Second Age]].

Their surviving ruins suggest a civilization far more technically and magically sophisticated than most people expect from early Sarosian humanity.

## Reputation

Legends credit the Storm Knights with mastery of:

- Esoteric [[./Fey-Lines|fey-line]] magic
- Fortification
- Strange mental or psionic disciplines
- Long-distance roads and outposts
- Tombs and protective complexes

How much of that reputation is accurate remains uncertain.

## Ruins

Storm Knight structures appear throughout Saros, often in places where no modern settlement could easily support such construction.

Some have become:

- Temples
- Forts
- Tombs
- Dungeons
- Roadside ruins
- Refuges used by later peoples

[[../../../Veiled Archive|Veiled Archive]] is one surviving complex associated with them.

## Fall

No complete account explains how the Storm Knights ended.

Their decline is tied broadly to the wars and political collapse remembered as the **Great Tribulation**.
