[[Fey-stone|Fey-stone]] is a rare dull-grey material found near powerful convergences of [[./Fey-Lines|Fey-Lines]].

In its raw state it can look unimpressive: a dense, malleable stone with little of the sparkle associated with ordinary precious gems.

Its value appears only when someone knows how to work it.

## Properties

Properly prepared Fey-stone can accept magical properties with unusual stability.

It has been used in:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Ritual tools
- Magical fittings
- Prosthetics
- Old dwarven machinery

## Working Fey-stone

The material is notoriously difficult to forge or shape without ruining its magical potential.

[[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/NPCs/Bedford|Bedford]] of [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/Locations/Bedford's_Forge|Bedford's Forge]] in [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/Goslow|Goslow]] is one of the few living craftspeople publicly known to work it reliably.

Older dwarven traditions, especially those associated with the [[../../Factions_& Clans/Dwarven Clans/Thar'um|Thar'um]], are believed to understand the material far better than most modern Sarosians.
