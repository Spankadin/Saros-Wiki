[[The_Scattering|The Scattering]] is the name given to the flight and dispersal of the surviving [[../../Factions_& Clans/Dwarven Clans/Khaz'armok|Khaz'armok]] houses after [[./The_Great_Betrayal|The Great Betrayal]].

It is distinct from [[./The_Wandering|The Wandering]]. The Wandering was the earlier expansion of the united [[../../Factions_& Clans/Dwarven Clans/Khaz'um|Khaz'um]] and the formation of the later [[../../Factions_& Clans/Dwarven Clans/Dwarven_Clans|Dwarven Clans]]. The Scattering was a forced diaspora caused by civil war, ruined holds, broken alliances, and the loss of safe deep roads.

Most accounts send the surviving houses toward [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/The_Glimmering_Expanse|The Glimmering Expanse]]. Whether they died there, passed through it, or established hidden settlements remains one of Saros's great dwarven mysteries.
