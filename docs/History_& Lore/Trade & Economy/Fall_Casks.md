**Fall casks** are heavily reinforced cargo barrels used to send durable goods from the high basin around [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/Waters/Lake_of_Lament|Lake of Lament]] down the otherwise unnavigable waterfall feeding the river toward [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]].

No ship can climb the cataract. A properly built cask can survive going over it.

The practice is dangerous, expensive, and inefficient compared with a road caravan, but it allows small quantities of dense or valuable cargo from the [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Lilya/Lilya|Lilya]] region to move south without first traveling through [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Trystn/Trystn|Trystn]].

## Construction

A proper fall cask is usually:

- Built from thick, slightly flexible timber
- Bound with multiple iron hoops
- Sealed internally with pitch and wax
- Packed tightly with straw, wool, sawdust, cloth, or fitted wooden braces
- Marked with a registered owner or sender brand
- Fitted with a durable consignment plate or waxed shipping tag
- Balanced so that it floats in a predictable orientation after the fall

The best cooperages charge heavily because a poor cask may arrive as scattered firewood.

## Common Cargo

Fall casks are best suited for:

- Metal ingots
- Ore samples
- Durable tools
- Stone carvings
- Pigments and mineral salts
- Sealed alchemical materials
- Dried herbs
- Small quantities of valuable, shock-resistant goods

People, animals, glassware, ordinary food cargo, and delicate manufactured goods generally travel by road.

## Recovery at Port Quartz

Catch crews at [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Locations/Cataract_Yard|Cataract Yard]] use chain booms, hooks, boats, winches, and recovery cranes to pull surviving casks from the river.

A recovered cask is logged before it is opened or released.

The recovery crew is entitled to a standard salvage and handling fee even when the rightful owner is known.

## Salvage and Claim Law

The [[../../Factions_& Clans/Mooring_Council|Mooring Council]] maintains strict claim rules because ownership disputes are nearly as common as broken casks.

### Registered cask with a living owner or named recipient

The cask remains the property of the registered owner or named consignee. It is released after identity, registration, recovery fees, and applicable customs are settled.

### Registered sender is deceased or missing

A named recipient still has the strongest claim.

If there is no named recipient, an acknowledged estate representative, business partner with documented authority, or insurer may submit a claim.

### Damaged mark or disputed ownership

The cask is moved into the **claim yard** at [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Locations/Cataract_Yard|Cataract Yard]].

Claimants must produce records strong enough for the salvage clerk or [[../../Factions_& Clans/Mooring_Council|Mooring Council]] to recognize a lawful interest.

### Unmarked or genuinely unclaimed cask

The town holds the cask for **thirty days**.

During that period it may not be sold, stripped, or privately opened except under a recognized safety exception.

If no valid claim is established by the end of the period, the Mooring Council may auction the cargo. Recovery fees and town charges are paid first.

### Altering a brand

Removing, burning over, or changing a registered cask mark is treated as deliberate cargo theft and is punished much more severely than ordinary scavenging.

## Opening a Held Cask

A held cask is normally kept sealed.

The civic authorities may order a supervised opening when there is a credible reason to believe the contents are:

- Leaking
- Dangerous
- Perishable in a way that threatens nearby cargo
- Magical and unstable
- Evidence in a serious crime
- Falsely presented as military or civic property

A disputed military claim does **not** automatically transfer ownership to [[../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|The Aegis Legion]]. Port Quartz's charter requires a lawful seizure, recognized emergency, or agreement with the Mooring Council.

## Local Saying

> “The fall decides what reaches Quartz. The Council decides whose it is.”
