[[Caelum|Caelum]] was a powerful human wizard from Mystara and the figure whose desperate wish began the modern history of [[../A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]].

## The Wish

Facing the destruction of his people, Caelum sought aid beyond anything ordinary magic could provide.

Tradition says he used **Contact Higher Plane** and reached a power identified in later accounts as Bahamut.

Caelum asked for his remaining people to be carried somewhere they could survive.

Nearly one thousand beings were transported to Saros.

Different traditions disagree about the exact price of the wish. One enduring account claims Caelum surrendered an unnatural immortality or the means of preserving it in exchange for his people's safety.

## King Caelum

Caelum became the central political figure of the newly arrived people.

His reputation combines:

- Powerful wizard
- Refugee leader
- Founder
- King
- Explorer
- Husband of [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/Queen_Amara|Queen Amara]]

He lived for centuries after the Arrival, an extraordinary lifespan even by magical standards.

## Caelum's End

Roughly eight hundred years ago, Caelum and [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/Queen_Amara|Queen Amara]] brought [[../../Factions_& Clans/The Prism/NPCs/Silas_Voss|Silas Voss]] to the northernmost foot-navigable point of Saros for one final attempt to turn their former pupil away from evil.

Only Amara witnessed the end. Caelum and Silas both went over the frozen edge into the sea below.

Caelum did not return. Silas eventually did.

Amara returned alone to [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/Pyrris|Pyrris]], where she assumed her husband's throne.

The place became known as [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/Caelum's_End|Caelum's End]].

What caused the fall, what Caelum intended, and what Amara saw remain among the great unanswered questions of Sarosian history.
