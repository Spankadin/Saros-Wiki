[[The_Serpent_King|The Serpent King]] was an ancient snake-man ruler whose underground domain once occupied part of the region south of modern [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/Goslow|Goslow]].

Later stories describe him as a fair and generous king besieged by enemies on several sides.

## Obsession

Despite his reputation as a capable ruler, the Serpent King spent an enormous share of his wealth seeking immortality.

He ordered his court wizard, [[./Xiximanter|Xiximanter]], to discover a reliable path to eternal life or a fountain of youth.

The search consumed years and resources.

## Death

The King died before Xiximanter completed his experiments.

His realm eventually collapsed and the underground city fell into ruin.

The site is now known as [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Dungeons/Tomb_of_The_Serpent_King|Tomb of The Serpent King]].

## Legacy

The irony of a ruler spending so much of his life trying not to die has made the Serpent King a favorite subject of priests, gamblers, and tavern philosophers throughout Goslow.
