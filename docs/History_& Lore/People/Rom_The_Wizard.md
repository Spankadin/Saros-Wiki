Legends tell of an old and exceedingly strange wizard called [[Rom_The_Wizard|Rom]] who has lived somewhere near the southern reaches of [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/Mountains/The_Masys_Mountains|The Masys Mountains]] for roughly **eighty years**.

The stories are unusually inconsistent even by Sarosian standards. Rom is described as a hermit, a prophet, a dangerous fool, an ancient magical scholar, and occasionally as someone who answers questions before they are asked.

One detail appears in several versions of the tale: Rom's home does not remain in one place. Some travelers call it a moving hill; others insist the hill bears gardens and lanterns.

## The Age of the Legend

There are **no centuries-old accounts** of Rom or a moving hill in the Masys region.

Old expedition journals such as those of [[./Edric_Vane|Edric Vane]] describe the terrain, rivers, mountain approaches, and dangers of the northwest but contain nothing resembling a giant snail or moving hill.

The first credible Rom stories begin only within the last eight decades.

## Port Quartz Stories

In [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]], Rom remains a frontier legend. Most people have never met anyone who can honestly claim to have seen him.

People who have traveled far beyond [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Locations/Westwater_Ferry|Westwater Ferry]] sometimes tell of:

- Strange healthy clearings in otherwise sick forest
- Impossible lights moving among the hills
- A distant rounded shape that is gone by morning
- Carefully placed stones bearing warnings no local scholar understands

None of those signs appear near Port Quartz itself.

## Reputation

Those who believe in Rom generally agree on one thing: if the magic of northern Saros is truly failing, he may be one of the few people strange enough to understand why.
