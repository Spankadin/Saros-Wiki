Sarosian historians divide the world's past into three broad Ages. The boundaries are partly historical and partly cultural; the farther back an Age lies, the less confidently anyone can separate record from legend.

## 1st Age — The First Breaths

There is no dependable written history from the First Age.

Tradition says dragons ruled before the younger peoples of Saros had established kingdoms of their own. The greatest surviving story tells of a catastrophic war among dragons powerful enough to permanently reshape the continent.

The clearest supposed evidence is central Saros:

- [[../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/The_Glimmering_Expanse|The Glimmering Expanse]], a vast desert of sand and jagged glass
- [[../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/The_Wyrm_Scar|The Wyrm Scar]], the enormous wound that nearly divides the continent in two

Whether every detail of the dragon war is true is unknowable. The land itself is difficult to explain any other way.

## 2nd Age — The Great Tribulation

The Second Age begins with the first humanoid civilizations remembered in surviving Sarosian tradition.

Magic was poorly understood by the earliest human tribes. Competition, mistrust, and betrayal eventually produced wars whose scale became legendary.

This Age saw:

- The rise and destruction of the [[./Legends/Storm_Knights|Storm Knights]]
- Wars among early human kingdoms
- The dead later associated with [[../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/Blackwater_Bog|Blackwater Bog]]
- The creation of the enormous burial mounds called [[../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/The_Urmong_Barrows|The Urmong Barrows]]
- Elven discovery and cultivation of the [[./Legends/Fey-Lines|Fey-Lines]]
- Elven withdrawal into hidden woodland domains
- The rise of the [[../Factions_& Clans/Dwarven Clans/Dwarven_Clans|Dwarven Clans]] and their great [[../Locations_& NPCs/Delves/Delves|Delves]]
- [[./Events/The_Great_Betrayal|The Great Betrayal]] and the later [[./Events/The_Scattering|Scattering]] of the Khaz'armok

By the end of the Age, organized human civilization had largely collapsed. Dwarven kingdoms had sealed or lost many delves. Elven populations had withdrawn from open political life. Survivors remained, but the old network of states and cities was gone.

## 3rd Age — Caelum's Arrival

Just over **1,400 years ago**, [[./People/Caelum|Caelum]]'s wish brought nearly one thousand refugees from Mystara to Saros.

Their arrival did not create civilization from nothing, but it transformed the scattered world they found.

The newcomers brought different methods of:

- Agriculture
- Fortification
- Warfare
- Record keeping
- Shipbuilding
- Trade
- Civic administration

They encountered surviving Sarosians living in isolated settlements, hidden communities, and the remnants of older cultures.

### The Years of Gathering

The first generations established defended settlements, cleared roads, recovered farmland, and made contact with surviving peoples.

The communities that would eventually become places such as [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/Goslow|Goslow]] and [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]] began to take recognizable form.

### The Chartering Centuries

Settlements expanded into towns. Trade routes stabilized. Guilds and civic charters appeared. The Crown's influence spread along roads, rivers, and ports rather than across every mile of wilderness.

### Caelum's End

Roughly eight centuries ago, Caelum and [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/Queen_Amara|Amara]] took their former pupil [[../Factions_& Clans/The Prism/NPCs/Silas_Voss|Silas Voss]] to the frozen northern peak now called [[../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/Caelum's_End|Caelum's End]] in one final attempt to turn him away from evil.

Only Amara witnessed what followed. Caelum and Silas both went over the edge into the sea. Neither has been reliably seen since.

Amara returned alone to [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/Pyrris|Pyrris]], where she assumed the throne of her husband.

The cause and manner of the fall, and the ultimate fate of both men, remain uncertain because Amara has never given a complete public account.

For a detailed population and settlement chronology of the Third Age, see [[./Sarosian_Timeline|Sarosian Timeline]].

### The Amaran Reign

Amara's eight-century reign brought long periods of comparative stability.

The settlements expanded, the [[../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]] became the principal military institution of the realm, civic charters matured, and trade became routine enough for ordinary people to build lives rather than merely survive.

The realm never fully conquered the wilderness.

Ruins, monsters, broken magical regions, old wars, pirates, and the sheer difficulty of travel kept Saros sparsely settled compared with the amount of land it claims.

### The Fraying Present

The Third Age has not officially ended.

[[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/Queen_Amara|Amara]] has been missing for months.

[[../Factions_& Clans/The Queen's Council/The_Queen's_Council|The Queen's Council]] governs in her absence. Legion officers possess growing local power. The [[../Factions_& Clans/The Black Circle/The_Black_Circle|Black Circle]] increasingly condemns Crown authority. Old factions are moving again.

Whether later historians will mark this period as the end of the Third Age has yet to be decided.
