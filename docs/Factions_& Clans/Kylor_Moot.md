The [[Kylor_Moot|Kylor Moot]] is the practical civic government of [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Kylor/Kylor|Kylor]].

Logging families, fishers, boat owners, timber factors, and experienced craftspeople dominate its decisions.

[[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Kylor/NPCs/Derran_Pike|Derran Pike]] serves as Harbor Reeve.

## Business

The Moot handles:

- Timber cutting rights
- Harbor rules
- Fishing disputes
- Fire precautions
- Coastal watch obligations
- Logging-road maintenance
- Contracts affecting communal forest access

Formal meetings are held when necessary.

Much of Kylor's real negotiation happens around [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Kylor/Locations/Saltwood_Market|Saltwood Market]], where everyone affected by a decision eventually has to do business.
