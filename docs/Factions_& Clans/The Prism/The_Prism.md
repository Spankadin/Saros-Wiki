[The Prism](The_Prism.md) is a secretive draconic cult associated with [Silas Voss](./NPCs/Silas_Voss.md) and his lieutenant [Malgrim Darkshade](./NPCs/Malgrim_Darkshade.md).

Most people in [Saros](../../History_&%20Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History.md) know the name only through rumor, warnings, recovered symbols, or stories about groups searching ancient ruins.

Fewer than one thousand true members are believed to exist, and even that is an uncertain upper limit. The cult's visible servants, hired agents, intermediaries, and sympathizers are not necessarily initiated members.

## The Multi-Chromatic Egg

The cult is obsessed with a Sarosian legend concerning the egg of a **Multi-Chromatic Dragon**.

The story claims that the first being seen by a newly hatched dragon of that kind will be granted a wish of extraordinary power.

The Prism believes the legend literally.

Prism prophecy claims that finding the egg will herald Silas's return, free him from the [Veil of Desolation](./NPCs/Silas_Voss.md#Immortality), and allow him to claim the throne of Saros. This is a statement of cult faith, not an established property of the egg or proof that Silas still lives.

## Methods

Public stories associate Prism agents with:

- Searching old ruins
- Studying dragons
- Stealing magical relics
- Recruiting dangerous spellcasters
- Buying information through intermediaries
- Operating through cult cells that do not always know one another
- Carrying small faceted crystals or using restrained prismatic signs

Because the cult is secretive, unrelated crimes are sometimes blamed on it simply because nobody has a better explanation.

## Appearance

Members favor restrained occult clothing rather than bright rainbow display: dark layered robes, practical hoods, matte fabrics, geometric embroidery, and faceted jewelry that reveals color mainly when it catches the light.

Witnesses sometimes describe a faint prismatic glint in a member's skin. Field agents usually hide obvious cult dress and may pass as scholars, travelers, merchants, or hired spellcasters.

## Silas Voss

[Silas Voss](./NPCs/Silas_Voss.md) is widely described as the cult's central figure.

His history with [Caelum](../../History_&%20Lore/People/Caelum.md) and [Queen Amara](../../Locations_&%20NPCs/Cities%20&%20Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/Queen_Amara.md) reaches back to Mystara and the catastrophe that preceded the Arrival.

The cult continues to recognize Silas as its true leader and reveres him with an intensity that often resembles worship. No reliable witness has seen him since [Caelum's End](../../Locations_&%20NPCs/Landmarks/Caelum's_End.md), however, and no one outside the cult can establish whether he survived the fall.

[Malgrim Darkshade](./NPCs/Malgrim_Darkshade.md) is the highest-ranking Prism operative publicly known to be active. Whether she receives instructions from Silas, interprets an older command, or governs in his absence is unknown.
