The [[Khaz'armok|Khaz'armok]] are the legendary **Sand Dwarves** and **Dune-Breakers**, a clan whose history became inseparable from [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Landmarks/The_Glimmering_Expanse|The Glimmering Expanse]]. The name now includes stone-shaping houses once recorded separately as the Khaz'dar.

## The Split

[[../../History_& Lore/Events/The_Great_Betrayal|The Great Betrayal]] began as a dispute among allied Khaz'armok houses over the finest gem their traditions had inherited from the older [[./Khaz'um|Khaz'um]]. Later outsiders treated two sides of the civil war as separate clans and called one of them Khaz'dar.

The feud shattered the old holds. During [[../../History_& Lore/Events/The_Scattering|The Scattering]], survivors moved into or through the Glimmering Expanse in search of safety and territory beyond their rivals.

## Craft

Khaz'armok tradition is associated with shaping earth and stone, excavating monumental chambers, building underground roads, and raising fortifications that look grown from the rock itself.

## Lost Routes

For a time, other Sarosians believed Khaz'armok guides knew how to cross sections of the Expanse that were otherwise nearly impassable.

Trade and communication eventually stopped.

## Modern Belief

Most people consider the clan lost.

Stories still claim that hidden Khaz'armok settlements survive among glass formations, buried fortresses, or deep desert delves.

No modern expedition has produced proof accepted by the wider realm.
