The [[Thar'um|Thar'um]] are the **Mountain Keepers** and **Gem-Seekers**, a [[./Dwarven_Clans|dwarven clan]] formed from traditions once recorded separately as the Tha'run and Vak'dar.

They are the dwarves most frequently seen in the settled parts of Saros, although Thar'um communities reveal themselves, open their gates, and trade only when they choose.

## Specialty

Thar'um craft focuses on:

- Gem cutting
- Magical stone
- Surveying fey-line convergences
- Deep mining
- [[../../History_& Lore/Legends/Fey-stone|Fey-stone]]
- Structural reinforcement
- Underground roads, ventilation, and water control
- Defensive gates and deliberate sealing

Dwarven tradition credits their forebears with developing many of the practices that later became known simply as **delving**. Their engineers are equally respected for knowing when a dangerous depth must be sealed.

## Reputation

Thar'um traders, engineers, and envoys are encountered throughout Saros, but outsiders rarely see the clan's deeper holdings. Stories about Thar'um works often end with a sealed door and no explanation.

Old Thar'um markings are especially prized because they can identify magical deposits, stable underground routes, or places that should never be opened again.
