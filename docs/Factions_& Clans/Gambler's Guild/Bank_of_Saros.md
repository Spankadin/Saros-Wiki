The [[Bank_of_Saros|Bank of Saros]] is not a separate chain of buildings. It is the banking network operated through every chartered [[./Gambler's_Guild|Gambler's Guild]] hall.

A depositor can place coin or valuables with one hall, receive a verified account mark or draft, and arrange a withdrawal through another. The Guild profits from fees, exchange rates, loans, storage, and the fact that merchants would rather trust its ledgers than carry a fortune through dangerous country.

## Services

Guild halls commonly provide:

- Coin deposits and withdrawals
- Secured storage for compact valuables
- Currency exchange and appraisal
- Verified drafts honored at another Guild hall
- Escrow for wagers and contracts
- Loans backed by property, cargo, or reputation

The Guild does not promise equal service to everyone. Large withdrawals may require notice, identity may be verified by private methods, and a disputed account can be frozen until Guild arbiters decide who is lying.

Every Bank of Saros is also a gambling hall. The Guild insists this concentrates security, trustworthy counting staff, and liquid coin in one place. Critics observe that it also places lenders beside people making poor decisions.
