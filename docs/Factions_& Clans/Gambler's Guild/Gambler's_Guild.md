The [Gambler's Guild](Gambler's_Guild.md) regulates and profits from organized gaming across much of civilized [Saros](../../History_&%20Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History.md).

Its influence is strongest in [Rore](../../Locations_&%20NPCs/Cities%20&%20Towns/Rore/Rore.md). The Guild charters exactly one official gambling hall in any city or town it serves. House games, tavern games, and street wagers may exist, but they are unsanctioned and receive no Guild protection.

Every chartered gambling hall also serves as a [Bank of Saros](./Bank_of_Saros.md), allowing depositors to move wealth between distant settlements without carrying all of it on the road.

## What the Guild Does

The Guild is concerned with:

- Recognized gaming rules
- House licensing
- Tournament organization
- Large wager verification
- Debt records
- Professional dealers
- Arbitration over disputed games
- Protection against obvious cheating
- Deposits, secured storage, currency exchange, and verified inter-hall drafts

The Guild's involvement does not make gambling safe or fair in every establishment. It means there is someone powerful enough to care when cheating damages the reputation of profitable gaming.

## Appearance and Habits

Guild professionals tend toward richer, cleaner, and more elaborate dress than ordinary merchants. Tailored coats, immaculate cuffs, fine waistcoats, polished jewelry, house tokens, cards, dice, and discreet banking seals all serve as signs that games and money are being handled under recognized authority.

The effect is controlled rather than gaudy. A successful dealer or banker wants every visible detail to suggest confidence, cleanliness, memory, and command of the room.

## Rore

In [Rore](../../Locations_&%20NPCs/Cities%20&%20Towns/Rore/Rore.md), the Guild is one of several institutions capable of influencing [The House Table](../The_House_Table.md).

It is wealthy, politically connected, and useful enough that even people who dislike gambling generally prefer it to a city full of entirely unregulated games.
