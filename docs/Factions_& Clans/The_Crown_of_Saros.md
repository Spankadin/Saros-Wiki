**The Crown of Saros** is the legal monarchy centered on [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/Queen_Amara|Queen Amara]] and [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/Pyrris|Pyrris]].

It is less a single office than the combined authority of the Queen, [[./The Queen's Council/The_Queen's_Council|The Queen's Council]], Crown magistrates, royal charters, and institutions such as [[./The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|The Aegis Legion]].

## Authority

The Crown claims authority over:

- Realm-wide law
- Coinage
- Major treaties
- High justice
- Military defense
- Royal charters
- Certain taxes and tariffs

Local settlements still govern much of their own daily life.

A town charter can therefore matter almost as much as a distant decree.

## Present Crisis

The disappearance of [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/Queen_Amara|Queen Amara]] has exposed how much legitimacy rested personally upon her.

The Council continues governing.

The Legion continues enforcing Crown law.

Local leaders increasingly ask who, exactly, has the right to expand those powers while the Queen is absent.
