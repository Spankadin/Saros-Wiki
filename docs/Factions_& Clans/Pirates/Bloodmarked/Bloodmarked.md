The [[Bloodmarked|Bloodmarked]] are one of the most feared pirate factions operating around southern [[../../../History_& Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]].

They are strongly associated with the waters near [[../../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Karastos/Karastos|Karastos]] and with attacks that draw the attention of [[../../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Grymswatch/Grymswatch|Grymswatch]] and the [[../../The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]] fleet.

## Reputation

Bloodmarked crews are known for aggressive boarding actions and for marking themselves or their ships in ways intended to make identification frightening rather than difficult.

Merchants often use the name broadly for violent raiders even when responsibility has not been proven.

## Organization

The Bloodmarked are not a single perfectly disciplined navy.

Individual captains possess substantial independence, bound together by shared reputation, alliances, and access to pirate markets.

That flexibility makes them difficult for [[../../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Grymswatch/NPCs/Admiral_Thainan|Admiral Thainan]] and other Legion commanders to eliminate through a single decisive campaign.
