The [[Stone_Council|Stone Council]] governs [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Lilya/Lilya|Lilya]] and represents the town’s mine crews, smelters, haulers, craftsmen, and established households.

[[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Lilya/NPCs/Maela_Torr|Maela Torr]] serves as Stone Reeve and chairs meetings at [[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Lilya/Locations/Stonewake_Hall|Stonewake Hall]].

The council cooperates with the [[./The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]] on defense but treats local mine law as a civic right rather than a privilege granted by the Crown.

## Authority

The Council sets mine closures, approves major hauling contracts, maintains emergency food and timber reserves, and settles disputes that cross several mining crews.

Its rulings matter because a careless decision underground can endanger an entire district rather than one household.

[[../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Lilya/NPCs/Maela_Torr|Maela Torr]] chairs meetings but is expected to persuade the other interests rather than simply command them.
