Major known [[./The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]] garrisons include:

- [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/Locations/Stonepeak|Stonepeak]] in [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/Pyrris|Pyrris]] — commanded by [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Pyrris/NPCs/General_Ironclad|General Ironclad]]
- [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/Locations/Duskpoint|Duskpoint]] outside [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/Goslow|Goslow]] — commanded by [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Goslow/NPCs/Vostin_The_Mighty|Vostin The Mighty]]
- [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Grymswatch/Locations/Mira's_Wall|Mira's Wall]] in [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Grymswatch/Grymswatch|Grymswatch]] — naval stronghold of [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Grymswatch/NPCs/Admiral_Thainan|Admiral Thainan]]
- The Port Quartz garrison — commanded by [[../../Locations_& NPCs/Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/NPCs/Captain_Serin_Holt|Captain Serin Holt]]

Smaller posts, road stations, signal towers, and temporary patrol camps exist throughout the settled regions of [[../../History_& Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]].

The Legion's legal authority differs according to local charter. A garrison may be militarily dominant without serving as the town's civil government.
