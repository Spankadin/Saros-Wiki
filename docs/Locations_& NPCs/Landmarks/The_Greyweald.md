[[The_Greyweald|The Greyweald]] is the broad, ashen forest east of [[./Mountains/The_Masys_Mountains|The Masys Mountains]], north of [[./Waters/Lake_of_Lament|Lake of Lament]], and west of [[./Mountains/The_Silvervein_Summits|The Silvervein Summits]]. It is both haunted and deeply altered by fey magic.

## The Bent Wood

Grey bark, silver moss, and leaves that show the wrong season give the forest its distant color. Within the trees, paths loop without crossing themselves, dawn can arrive twice, and a traveler's echo may answer before the traveler speaks.

The corruption is uneven. A glade may be harmless for decades and become dangerous in a single night. Elsewhere, a poisoned-looking grove shelters clean water while green and welcoming ground quietly leads people astray.

## Hauntings

Travelers report:

- Lanterns carried by people who cast no shadow
- Campsites found warm and empty, then found again miles away
- Lost companions speaking from behind trees they could not have reached
- Processions of old dead who ignore the living unless addressed
- Fey revels that leave guests days or years from where they entered

Some spirits appear to be human dead. Others imitate human memory without understanding it.

## Travel Customs

Experienced travelers mark both sides of a tree, distrust straight trails, and never follow a familiar voice they cannot see. Iron bells, bread left at a fork, and sleeping with boots pointed outward are common precautions, though no custom works everywhere.

The Greyweald is not a single curse with a known center. Scholars suspect overlapping [[../../History_& Lore/Legends/Fey-Lines|Fey-Lines]], old violence, and centuries of isolation have created many smaller hauntings that now behave like one wilderness.
