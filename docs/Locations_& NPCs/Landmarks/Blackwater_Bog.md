[[Blackwater_Bog|Blackwater Bog]] is an enormous southern wetland whose dark water, drowned trees, and unstable ground conceal one of the great mass graves of the [[../../History_& Lore/Ages_of_Saros|Second Age]].

## The Dead

Stories of the **Great Tribulation** describe armies dying here in numbers too large to bury properly.

Bodies were piled into low ground, thrown into water, covered by mud, or simply abandoned where they fell.

Centuries of peat and black water have hidden much of the battlefield without truly consuming it.

Bones, weapons, armor, and old banners still surface after floods.

## Travel

The Bog is dangerous even without ghosts.

Hazards include:

- False ground
- Deep mud
- Sudden channels
- Insects
- Poisonous plants
- Rotting trees
- Poor visibility
- Creatures adapted to the wetland

Old causeways appear on some maps and vanish on others.

## Reputation

The water is often described as unnaturally black.

Locals disagree whether the color comes from peat, ancient blood, magic, or all three.

Travelers tell stories of drowned soldiers walking beneath the surface and of voices calling from places where no solid ground exists.
