[[The_Urmong_Barrows|The Urmong Barrows]] are a vast field of ancient burial mounds in southern Saros, raised during the wars of the [[../../History_& Lore/Ages_of_Saros|Second Age]].

Some are ordinary earthen tombs.

Others are so large that travelers mistake them for natural hills.

The greatest rise like small mountains.

## Origin

Tradition connects the Barrows to the worst human conflicts of the **Great Tribulation**.

Entire armies were buried rather than returned home, and later generations continued piling earth, stone, weapons, and dead atop older graves.

No surviving record agrees on how many bodies lie beneath the region.

## Reputation

The Barrows are widely considered haunted.

Travelers report:

- Lights moving between mounds
- Voices beneath sealed stone
- Empty armor standing upright at dawn
- Animals refusing certain valleys
- Old weapons appearing on the surface after storms
- Paths returning travelers to places they already passed

Many stories are probably exaggerations.

Enough are not.

## Ani

The fortified coastal town of [[../Cities & Towns/Ani/Ani|Ani]] exists partly because settled southern communities do not trust the Barrows to remain quiet.

Expeditions enter the region from Ani regularly.

Some return with old treasure.

Some return with fewer people.

Some do not return.
