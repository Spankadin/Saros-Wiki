[[The_High_Road|The High Road]] is the enormous switchback road climbing from the [[../../Cities & Towns/Goslow/Goslow|Goslow]] region to [[../../Cities & Towns/Pyrris/Pyrris|Pyrris]] atop the cliffs above [[../Waters/Bishop's_Bay|Bishop's Bay]].

It is the only normal public route into the capital.

## Travel

The climb is long enough that a journey between Goslow and Pyrris can consume most of a day even under good conditions.

Loaded wagons move more slowly.

Bad weather, inspections, damaged road sections, or heavy traffic can extend the trip considerably.

## Security

The road is strategically vital and closely watched.

Travelers pass Legion checkpoints associated with [[../../Cities & Towns/Pyrris/Locations/Stonepeak|Stonepeak]], and dangerous sections are protected by walls, gates, and reinforced turns.

Closing the High Road would isolate Pyrris from the supply network it depends upon.
