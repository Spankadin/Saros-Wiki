The [[Surveyors'_Step|Surveyors' Step]] is the collective name used in [[../../Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]] for the fading trails, cairns, hunter paths, and old expedition cuts beyond [[../../Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Locations/Westwater_Ferry|Westwater Ferry]].

It is not a true road.

The Step only describes the first portion of the northwest route where generations of travelers have left enough signs that one can pretend a path exists.

A cracked expedition marker connected to [[../../../History_& Lore/People/Edric_Vane|Edric Vane]] stands near the last dependable portion.

Beyond that point, travelers heading toward [[../Mountains/The_Masys_Mountains|The Masys Mountains]] are navigating wilderness rather than following a road.
