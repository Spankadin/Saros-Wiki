[[Lake_of_Lament|Lake of Lament]] occupies the high basin around [[../Mountains/The_Silvervein_Summits|The Silvervein Summits]].

Beyond its northern shore lies [[../The_Greyweald|The Greyweald]], whose grey treeline fills the country between the Silvervein Summits and [[../Mountains/The_Masys_Mountains|The Masys Mountains]].

It sits far above the elevation of [[../../Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]], fed by mountain water and drained through several channels.

## The Great Fall

At the lake's southwestern edge, the water plunges over an enormous waterfall before becoming part of the river system flowing south toward [[../../Cities & Towns/Port Quartz/Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]].

The fall makes ship travel between Port Quartz and the lake impossible.

No vessel can climb it.

The violent downstream route is nevertheless used for one unusual kind of cargo: reinforced [[../../../History_& Lore/Trade & Economy/Fall_Casks|Fall Casks]].

## Trade

Communities around [[../../Cities & Towns/Lilya/Lilya|Lilya]] can send small amounts of durable goods down the cataract in specially built casks.

Most normal freight still travels by road through [[../../Cities & Towns/Trystn/Trystn|Trystn]].

## Reputation

The lake is beautiful from a distance and dangerous at its edges.

Mountain weather can change water conditions quickly, and the outlet currents become increasingly violent toward the waterfall.
