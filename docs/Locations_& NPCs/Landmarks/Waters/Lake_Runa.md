[[Lake_Runa|Lake Runa]] is a southern inland lake surrounded by dense forest and old halfling traditions.

Families in [[../../Cities & Towns/Mysha/Mysha|Mysha]] preserve stories connecting their ancestors to the lake and treat it as more than a simple source of water.

## Character

Runa's shoreline is heavily wooded, with stretches difficult to reach by road.

Fishing, herbs, small boats, and forest travel are more important here than large-scale commerce.

## Tradition

Halfling stories disagree on whether the lake was a refuge, birthplace, gathering place, or all three.

Many households in Mysha still preserve small customs associated with journeys to Runa.
