[[The_Wyrm_Scar|The Wyrm Scar]] is the greatest visible wound in the geography of [[../../History_& Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]].

The enormous canyon and watercourse nearly divide the continent into northern and southern halves, connecting the waters of [[./Waters/The_Sea_of_Myth|The Sea of Myth]] and [[./Waters/The_Sparkling_Sea|The Sparkling Sea]] through a broken central region.

## First Age Tradition

The Scar and [[./The_Glimmering_Expanse|The Glimmering Expanse]] are traditionally attributed to the dragon wars of the [[../../History_& Lore/Ages_of_Saros|First Age]].

The scale of the canyon is one reason even skeptical historians hesitate to dismiss the stories entirely.

## Passage

Travel through or around the Scar is extremely difficult.

Glass, broken rock, violent water, sheer walls, and unstable routes make ordinary navigation dangerous.

A land bridge exists across the greater divide, but it is surrounded by the Glimmering Expanse. Reaching it is usually more dangerous than the crossing itself.

For practical purposes, Saros's northern and southern civilizations are connected by long routes around the central danger rather than a dependable road across it.
