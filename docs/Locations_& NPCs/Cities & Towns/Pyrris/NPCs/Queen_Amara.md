[[Queen_Amara|Queen Amara]] Tatami is the elven Queen of [[../../../../History_& Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]], widow of [[../../../../History_& Lore/People/Caelum|Caelum]], and one of the longest-lived rulers known to the realm.

She has ruled for roughly **eight hundred years**.

## Appearance and Presence

Amara is remembered as an ethereal and regal figure whose presence seems touched by residual magic. Her long life, elven heritage, and considerable arcane power have made her as much a living historical symbol as a monarch.

## Reputation

For most of her reign, Amara was regarded as:

- Benevolent
- Patient
- Forgiving
- Personally kind
- Difficult for ordinary people to reach
- Strangely distant from smaller political disputes

People from across Saros have traditionally traveled to [[../Pyrris|Pyrris]] hoping to bring petitions or serious grievances before the Queen.

Her willingness to forgive made her beloved by many and frustrating to officials who wanted harsher punishment.

## The Council

[[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Queen's Council/The_Queen's_Council|The Queen's Council]] has handled much of the realm's daily administration for centuries.

It has long been an open secret that the Council exercises more practical control over taxation, military policy, diplomacy, intelligence, finance, and magic than the Queen's public image suggests.

Amara's authority nevertheless gave the system legitimacy that the Council alone does not possess.

## Caelum's End

Amara and [[../../../../History_& Lore/People/Caelum|Caelum]] made one final attempt to turn their former pupil [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Prism/NPCs/Silas_Voss|Silas Voss]] away from evil at [[../../../Landmarks/Caelum's_End|Caelum's End]].

No one else witnessed it. Caelum and Silas both went over the frozen edge into the northern sea.

Amara returned alone to [[../Pyrris|Pyrris]], where she assumed her husband's throne. She has never provided a complete public account of the fall, and no accepted evidence proves that either Caelum or Silas survived it.

## Disappearance

Amara has now been missing for months.

No public explanation has satisfied the realm.

Common theories claim she has been:

- Imprisoned within [[../Locations/The_Radiant_Citadel|The Radiant Citadel]]
- Kidnapped
- Overthrown by [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Queen's Council/The_Queen's_Council|the Council]]
- Taken by [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Prism/The_Prism|The Prism]]
- Killed
- Forced into hiding by an unknown threat

The Council continues to govern in her name.
