[[General_Ironclad|General Roland Ironclad]] is the supreme military commander of [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|The Aegis Legion]] and the military voice on [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Queen's Council/The_Queen's_Council|The Queen's Council]].

He resides and commands from [[../Locations/Stonepeak|Stonepeak]] in [[../Pyrris|Pyrris]].

## Appearance

Roland is an imposing, weathered man with a close-cropped beard and the posture of someone who expects rooms to organize themselves when he enters.

His military dress is immaculate without being decorative.

## Manner

Ironclad is disciplined, strategic, and rarely hurried in public. He listens longer than many people expect and then gives instructions with very little wasted language.

He takes the Legion's motto—**Honor, Heart, Homeland**—seriously.

## Responsibility

As supreme commander, Ironclad oversees:

- Realm defense
- Legion doctrine
- Garrisons
- Strategic patrols
- Senior officer appointments
- Responses to major military threats

Officers such as [[../../Goslow/NPCs/Vostin_The_Mighty|Vostin The Mighty]], [[../../Grymswatch/NPCs/Admiral_Thainan|Admiral Thainan]], and [[../../Port Quartz/NPCs/Captain_Serin_Holt|Captain Serin Holt]] ultimately serve within the institution he commands.

## Current Position

The disappearance of [[./Queen_Amara|Queen Amara]] has made Ironclad one of the most consequential people in Saros.

He publicly supports continuity of government through the Council and has not declared the realm under military rule.
