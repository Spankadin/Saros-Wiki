[[The_Radiant_Citadel|The Radiant Citadel]] is the royal seat of [[../../../../History_& Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]] and the dominant structure of [[../Pyrris|Pyrris]].

The enormous white tower rises from the cliffside high above [[../../../Landmarks/Waters/Bishop's_Bay|Bishop's Bay]] and can be seen from great distances in clear weather.

## Purpose

The Citadel contains:

- The royal household
- Chambers of [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Queen's Council/The_Queen's_Council|The Queen's Council]]
- Court offices
- Records and archives
- Guest quarters for dignitaries
- Ceremonial halls
- Protected magical and administrative spaces

[[../NPCs/Queen_Amara|Queen Amara]] traditionally resides here.

## Fortification

The Citadel stands within powerful walls and is guarded by the Crown and [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]].

Its height, cliff position, restricted approaches, and controlled access make it one of the most defensible inhabited places in Saros.

## Public Access

Most visitors to Pyrris never see the majority of the Citadel.

Petitioners, officials, messengers, and dignitaries are admitted only to the sections relevant to their business.
