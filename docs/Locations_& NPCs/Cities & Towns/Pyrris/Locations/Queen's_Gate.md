[[Queen's_Gate|Queen's Gate]] is the guarded waterline approach beneath the cliffs of [[../Pyrris|Pyrris]]. It is part landing, part lifting station, and part defensive checkpoint serving royal and military needs below [[./The_Radiant_Citadel|The Radiant Citadel]].

It is not a public harbor or a convenient shortcut into the capital. Ordinary commercial vessels passing through [[../../../Landmarks/Waters/Bishop's_Bay|Bishop's Bay]] keep clear of the restricted waters while Crown and [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]] patrols watch the approach.

The principal public route into Pyrris remains [[../../../Landmarks/Roads/The_High_Road|The High Road]].
