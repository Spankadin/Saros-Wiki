[Pyrris](Pyrris.md) is the capital of [Saros](../../../History_&%20Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History.md), but it is not a large conventional city. It is a fortified royal settlement built atop an immense sheer cliff overlooking [Bishop's Bay](../../Landmarks/Waters/Bishop's_Bay.md), centered almost entirely around [The Radiant Citadel](./Locations/The_Radiant_Citadel.md).

Only about eleven hundred people reside there permanently. Most are members of the royal household, [Council](../../../Factions_&%20Clans/The%20Queen's%20Council/The_Queen's_Council.md) staff, scribes, diplomats, priests, Legion officers, servants, craftspeople, and the families required to keep the capital operating.

Visitors can make the population swell dramatically during festivals, petitions, royal hearings, and times of crisis.

## Character

Pyrris is formal, vertical, windy, and inconvenient.

Space within the walls is precious. Food must be brought in, water carefully stored, and almost every permanent resident has some relationship to the Crown or the Citadel.

For ordinary Sarosians, visiting Pyrris is often a once-in-a-lifetime journey made to petition [Queen Amara](./NPCs/Queen_Amara.md), seek judgment, attend ceremony, or witness [The Spring Harvest Festival](../../../History_&%20Lore/Events/The_Spring_Harvest_Festival.md).

## Access

The only normal public approach is [The High Road](../../Landmarks/Roads/The_High_Road.md), a massive switchback road climbing from the direction of [Goslow](../Goslow/Goslow.md). The ascent can consume most of a travel day even when the road is open and weather is favorable.

Pyrris is heavily dependent on Goslow for food, timber, manufactured goods, travelers, and access to the wider trade network.

The cliffs also descend directly into Bishop's Bay. Crown and Legion patrols maintain restricted waters below the capital, and ordinary vessels are kept well away from protected royal infrastructure.

[Queen's Gate](./Locations/Queen's_Gate.md) is the guarded waterline approach below the cliffs. It serves royal and military needs and is not a public harbor.

## Travel and Supply

The High Road route from [Goslow](../Goslow/Goslow.md) is approximately **35-40 miles**. It takes about 2 days on foot, 2-3 days by loaded wagon, or 1 very long day to 2 days mounted. A griffin can make the journey in roughly 2-3 hours.

Food arriving from Goslow includes grain, vegetables, livestock products, and preserved seafood from [Bishop's Bay](../../Landmarks/Waters/Bishop's_Bay.md). The cost and difficulty of moving every ordinary necessity up the cliff are major reasons Pyrris remains a small fortress-capital.

See [Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population](../../../History_&%20Lore/Trade%20&%20Economy/Saros_-_Distances,_Travel,_Trade,_and_Population.md) for the canonical route model.

## Government

Pyrris is the seat of [Queen Amara](./NPCs/Queen_Amara.md) and [Council](../../../Factions_&%20Clans/The%20Queen's%20Council/The_Queen's_Council.md).

Since the Queen's disappearance, the Council has continued issuing decrees in her name while [General Ironclad](./NPCs/General_Ironclad.md) and the [Aegis Legion](../../../Factions_&%20Clans/The%20Aegis%20Legion/The_Aegis_Legion.md) maintain order.

The city's small permanent population means the capital functions more like an enormous royal fortress and administrative center than a self-sustaining urban economy.

## Security

- The Citadel walls and inner guards
- [Stonepeak](./Locations/Stonepeak.md), commanded by [General Ironclad](./NPCs/General_Ironclad.md)
- Checkpoints controlling the High Road
- Restricted air and water approaches
- Legion patrols around the cliff and bay

## Places of Interest

- [The Radiant Citadel](./Locations/The_Radiant_Citadel.md) — the great white tower and royal seat
- [Stonepeak](./Locations/Stonepeak.md) — Legion garrison and primary public checkpoint
- [The High Road](../../Landmarks/Roads/The_High_Road.md) — the public road connecting Pyrris to the Goslow region
