[[Stonewake_Hall|Stonewake Hall]] is the civic meeting house of [[../Lilya|Lilya]] and the seat of [[../NPCs/Maela_Torr|Maela Torr]] and the [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/Stone_Council|Stone Council]].

The hall is built against the mountain itself. Its rear wall is exposed stone covered with old survey marks and memorial names.

Mine disputes, ration decisions, road contracts, and emergency closures are argued here.

## Local Importance

The place is a familiar part of daily life and a useful point of reference for visitors. Its value comes from the ordinary work, meetings, and routines that happen there rather than from a single famous event.
