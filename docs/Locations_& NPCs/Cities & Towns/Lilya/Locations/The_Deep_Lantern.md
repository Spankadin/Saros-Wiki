[[The_Deep_Lantern|The Deep Lantern]] is the largest inn and miners’ hiring hall in [[../Lilya|Lilya]].

Prospectors, haulers, surveyors, and off-shift miners exchange contracts and rumors here.

[[../NPCs/Ysra_Pell|Ysra Pell]] is a regular, and recent talk increasingly concerns dead herbs, sealed lower works, and strange lights beneath [[../../../Landmarks/Mountains/The_Silvervein_Summits|The Silvervein Summits]].

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.
