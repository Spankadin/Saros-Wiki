[[Maela_Torr|Maela Torr]] is the current Stone Reeve of [[../Lilya|Lilya]] and chairs the [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/Stone_Council|Stone Council]].

She comes from a family of mine surveyors rather than mine owners and has a reputation for knowing exactly how much ore, food, timber, and labor the town can spare before a promise becomes impossible.

Maela respects [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|The Aegis Legion]] when it keeps roads and mine entrances safe, but she opposes military interference in local mine law.

The recent problems beneath [[../../../Landmarks/Mountains/The_Silvervein_Summits|The Silvervein Summits]] have made her increasingly suspicious of sealed orders arriving from outside Lilya.

## Manner

Maela dislikes promises that do not include numbers. A plan that cannot explain how much timber, food, rope, labor, or time it requires is not yet a plan in her view.
