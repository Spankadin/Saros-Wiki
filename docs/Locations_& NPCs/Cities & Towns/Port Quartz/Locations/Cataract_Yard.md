# Cataract Yard

The Cataract Yard is the fortified fall-cask recovery complex above the main quays.

It contains:
- Chain booms
- Recovery cranes
- Winches
- Sorting platforms
- Cooperages
- Drying sheds
- Inspection warehouses
- A salvage court

Catch crews recover reinforced barrels sent down the great waterfall from the Lake of Lament basin.

After storms, the Yard may receive casks, trees, animal carcasses, wreckage, and debris at the same time.
