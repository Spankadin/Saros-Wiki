[[Cataract_Yard|Cataract Yard]] is the [[../../../../History_& Lore/Trade & Economy/Fall_Casks|fall-cask]] recovery and salvage works of [[../Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]].

It sits along a heavily reinforced stretch of river equipped with chain booms, recovery hooks, cranes, winches, sorting platforms, cooperages, and guarded storage.

Goods deliberately sent over the great waterfall from the region around [[../../../Landmarks/Waters/Lake_of_Lament|Lake of Lament]] are recovered here when they survive the journey.

See [[../../../../History_& Lore/Trade & Economy/Fall_Casks|fall-cask]] for the full trade and ownership rules.

## Recovery

Catch crews intercept surviving casks before they continue too far downstream.

Every recovered cask is:

1. Hauled onto a numbered platform
2. Recorded by brand and condition
3. Checked for obvious leaks or hazards
4. Assessed for recovery fees
5. Released, forwarded, or moved into claim storage

## Claim Yard

The guarded **claim yard** holds casks whose ownership cannot immediately be resolved.

Common reasons include:

- Damaged brands
- Missing or deceased senders
- Absent named recipients
- Competing claims
- Unmarked cargo
- Suspicious or contradictory papers

Under Port Quartz law, genuinely unclaimed casks are normally held for **thirty days** before the [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/Mooring_Council|Mooring Council]] can authorize auction.

A cask may be opened early under civic supervision when it is leaking, unstable, dangerous, or evidence in a serious dispute.

## Atmosphere

After storms, Cataract Yard can become chaotic. Casks arrive mixed with branches, wreckage, dead animals, and whatever else the river has decided to surrender.
