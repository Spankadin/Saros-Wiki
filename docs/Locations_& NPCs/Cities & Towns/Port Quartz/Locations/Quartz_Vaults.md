# Quartz Vaults

The Quartz Vaults are secure warehouses cut into pale stone above the quays.

Upper chambers hold ordinary high-value cargo. Lower chambers are older than the current town, and several sealed doors have resisted every local locksmith.

The Mooring Council keeps separate chambers for confiscated cargo, emergency grain, civic records, and dangerous salvage.
