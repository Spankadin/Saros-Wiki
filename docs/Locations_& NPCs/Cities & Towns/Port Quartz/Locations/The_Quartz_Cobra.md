[[The_Quartz_Cobra|The Quartz Cobra]] is [[../Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]]'s sole [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/Gambler's Guild/Gambler's_Guild|Guild-chartered]] gambling hall and its Bank of Saros.

Pale stone scales curve around the doorway, while a long quartz cobra coils above the counting cage inside. [[../NPCs/Lind_Bergy|Lind Bergy]] owns and operates the hall.

## Gambling

The Cobra favors compact games suited to sailors, caravan crews, and merchants waiting on a ferry. Declared odds and stakes are written where all participants can see them. High-value private rooms overlook the river without providing a route to it.

## Banking

The secured lower rooms accept deposits, store valuables, honor verified Guild drafts, exchange coin, and arrange withdrawals through other Guild halls. River schedules make the hall especially valuable to merchants who do not want their entire payroll sitting on a delayed wagon.
