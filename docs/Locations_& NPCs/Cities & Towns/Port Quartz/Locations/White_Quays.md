# White Quays

The White Quays are Port Quartz’s principal waterfront, carved into broad shelves of pale quartz.

When wet, the stone reflects lantern light and appears faintly luminous.

Daily activity includes:
- Ferry loading
- Cargo weighing
- Customs inspections
- Rivercraft auctions
- Animal handling
- Ship and barge repair
- Public arguments over salvage and tariffs

A signal bell system announces hazards, arrivals, closures, broken lines, and bodies recovered from the river.
