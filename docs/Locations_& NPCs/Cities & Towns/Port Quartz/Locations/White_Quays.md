The [[White_Quays|White Quays]] are the principal commercial waterfront of [[../Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]], carved into broad shelves of pale quartz.

Rain and river spray make the stone shine beneath lantern light, giving arriving travelers one of the town's most distinctive views.

## Activity

The quays handle:

- Passenger ferries
- Cargo loading
- Customs inspections
- Rivercraft repair
- Auctions
- Animal transfers
- Warehousing
- Catch crews delivering recovered goods from [[./Cataract_Yard|Cataract Yard]]

Signal bells from [[./Chainwatch|Chainwatch]] coordinate ferry movements and warn crews about dangerous debris or sudden closures.

## Local Importance

The White Quays are public ground and one of the few places where merchants, laborers, civic officials, Legion soldiers, river crews, and foreign travelers constantly mix.
