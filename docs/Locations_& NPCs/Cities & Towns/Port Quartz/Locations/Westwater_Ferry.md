[[Westwater_Ferry|Westwater Ferry]] is the principal western river crossing at [[../Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]].

Heavy wagon ferries, smaller passenger boats, a fortified watch post, stables, equipment sellers, and a missing-person board surround the landing.

[[../NPCs/Oren_Bale|Oren Bale]] is the senior ferrymaster.

## Routes

Travelers arriving from [[../../Goslow/Goslow|Goslow]] or [[../../Kylor/Kylor|Kylor]] may use Westwater as part of ordinary regional travel.

People turning northwest toward [[../../../Landmarks/Mountains/The_Masys_Mountains|The Masys Mountains]] also cross here, but the ferry should not be mistaken for the start of a safe road.

The last fragments of maintained path soon give way to the trails collectively known as [[../../../Landmarks/Roads/Surveyors'_Step|Surveyors' Step]].

## The Vane Marker

A weathered marker commemorating [[../../../../History_& Lore/People/Edric_Vane|Edric Vane]] points northwest.

The marker is older than the modern legends of [[../../../../History_& Lore/People/Rom_The_Wizard|Rom]] and a moving hill, and reflects Vane's historical expedition route rather than either story.

## Practical Rule

Westwater can place a traveler on the correct bank.

What happens after that is no longer the ferrymaster's promise.
