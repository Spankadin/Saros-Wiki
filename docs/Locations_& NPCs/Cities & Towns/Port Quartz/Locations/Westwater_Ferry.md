# Westwater Ferry

Westwater is the principal crossing for travelers arriving from Goslow and Kylor or departing toward the unsettled northwest.

The landing includes:
- Heavy wagon ferries
- Passenger boats
- A fortified watch post
- Stables
- Equipment sellers
- A return shrine
- Missing-person notices
- A marker honoring Edric Vane

The marker points northwest even though no maintained road continues in that direction.
