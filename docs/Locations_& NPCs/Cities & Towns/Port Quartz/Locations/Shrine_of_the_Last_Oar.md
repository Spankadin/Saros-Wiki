The [[Shrine_of_the_Last_Oar|Shrine of the Last Oar]] is a small riverside sanctuary in [[../Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]] dedicated to people lost upon the water.

Broken oars, carved names, recovered possessions, river stones, and family offerings fill the interior.

## Neutrality

The shrine does not distinguish between Crown sailors, pirates, merchants, ferrymen, [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Black Circle/The_Black_Circle|Black Circle]] travelers, or ordinary dead.

That neutrality is respected strongly enough that even people who dislike one another may leave messages or arrange quiet meetings nearby.

> “Grief has no faction.”

## Local Importance

The place is a familiar part of daily life and a useful point of reference for visitors. Its value comes from the ordinary work, meetings, and routines that happen there rather than from a single famous event.
