# Shrine of the Last Oar

A small riverside sanctuary dedicated to those lost upon the water.

The interior contains carved names, broken oars, river stones, recovered possessions, and travelers’ offerings.

The shrine does not distinguish between Crown sailors, pirates, Black Circle travelers, merchants, or ordinary dead. Its neutrality makes it a quiet place for messages and meetings.

> “Grief has no faction.”
