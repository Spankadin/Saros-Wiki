[[Prance_Mondrel|Prance Mondrel]] owns and operates [[../Locations/The_Fourty_Knights|The Fourty Knights]], the largest tavern and inn on the docks of [[../Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]].

Prance is famously lazy. He spends a large portion of each day in an oversized chair directing servants, complaining about expenses, and explaining to customers why whatever insect they just saw is either harmless, temporary, or technically someone else's responsibility.

## Personality

Prance is loud, shameless, and much more observant than his behavior suggests.

He dislikes physical work but has an excellent memory for faces, tabs, room numbers, arguments, and who arrived in town with whom.

## Business

The Fourty Knights succeeds because Prance understands travelers.

He keeps enough rooms, food, drink, stable arrangements, and hired help available that merchants and expedition parties tolerate the tavern's rough edges.

Prance is a member of the [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/Merchant's Guild/Merchant's_Guild|Merchant's Guild]], though his idea of respectable commerce is broader than some guild members would prefer.
