# Caldus Renn

Caldus Renn is one of the few living guides known to have crossed the western rivers and returned from the Masys foothills.

He no longer accepts commissions.

Caldus walks with a stiff left leg and wears river stones tied into his bootlaces. He claims they remind him which side of a crossing he entered from.

## His final expedition

Caldus found:
- Corrupted woodland
- A circle of healthy flowers
- A deliberate stone arrangement
- A distant rounded shape gone the next day

Two companions vanished after following lights uphill.

## What he provides

- The safest known secondary-river crossing
- A warning about misleading high-ground routes
- The last useful cairn
- Advice that healthy magical pockets are better guides than old roads

He will speak if Hesta sends the party, they show the corruption sample, Bastet manifests, or they promise to record what happened to the missing.
