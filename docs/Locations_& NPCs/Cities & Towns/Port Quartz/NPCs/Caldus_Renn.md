[[Caldus_Renn|Caldus Renn]] is one of the few living guides known to have crossed the western rivers and returned from the foothills of [[../../../Landmarks/Mountains/The_Masys_Mountains|The Masys Mountains]].

He no longer accepts commissions.

Caldus walks with a stiff left leg and ties small river stones into his bootlaces. He claims they remind him which side of a crossing he entered from.

## His Final Expedition

Within the last few decades, Caldus found corrupted woodland, a circle of inexplicably healthy flowers, and a distant rounded shape that was gone the following morning.

Two companions vanished after following lights uphill.

Caldus does not claim to have met [[../../../../History_& Lore/People/Rom_The_Wizard|Rom]], but his account is one of the stronger recent pieces of evidence that something strange moves through the Masys country.
