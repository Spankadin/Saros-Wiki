# Oren Bale

Oren Bale is the senior ferrymaster at [[../Locations/Westwater_Ferry|Westwater Ferry]].

He decides when wagons, animals, and expeditions may cross the main river.

Oren is patient with fear and impatient with bravado.

He asks every northwest-bound traveler:
- How many days of food they carry
- Whether they can repair a boat
- Whether they know the water level
- Who should be told if they do not return

He knows where the maintained path ends, which landing avoids marsh ground, and how rain changes the secondary crossings.

> “Once the river is behind you, every trail is somebody’s guess.”
