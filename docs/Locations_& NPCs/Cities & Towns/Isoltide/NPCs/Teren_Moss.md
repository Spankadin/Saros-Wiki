[[Teren_Moss|Teren Moss]] serves as East Reeve of [[../Isoltide|Isoltide]].

A former timber factor with deep family ties to the lakeshore, Teren is comfortable negotiating with ferrymen, fishers, foresters, and merchants who all believe their problems should be solved first.

Teren has a friendly public rivalry with [[../../Albyrt/NPCs/Mara_Avelin|Mara Avelin]] of [[../../Albyrt/Albyrt|Albyrt]]. Their arguments over tolls and ferry schedules are famous enough to be repeated in taverns, but both understand that the twin cities depend on one another.

## Reputation

Teren's rivalry with [[../../Albyrt/NPCs/Mara_Avelin|Mara Avelin]] of [[../../Albyrt/Albyrt|Albyrt]] is real but mostly civic theater. Both leaders understand that a serious failure of ferry service or lake security hurts both cities at once.
