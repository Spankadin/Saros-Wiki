[[The_Green_Lantern|The Green Lantern]] sits near the forest road into [[../Isoltide|Isoltide]]. Foresters, ferrymen, and travelers use it as a meeting point before deciding whether to continue by road or cross the lake.

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.

## Waypoint

The Green Lantern is commonly used as a staging point for travelers deciding between the forest road and lake passage.

Stable space, packed meals, local weather advice, and introductions to ferry crews are as important to the business as beds.
