[[Mara_Sedge|Mara Sedge]] is the Council Speaker and official grain assessor of [[../Skaggerty|Skaggerty]]. She chairs the village council from [[../Locations/The_Crossroads_Weighhouse|The Crossroads Weighhouse]], settles routine disputes, certifies wagon weights, and keeps the stores that matter when a harvest fails.

## Work

Mara's ordinary responsibilities include:

- Calling and moderating council meetings
- Maintaining charter, tax, and road records
- Certifying grain weights and resolving market disputes
- Coordinating palisade repairs and militia watches
- Releasing emergency grain by council vote
- Speaking for Skaggerty to merchants, Crown officers, and nearby settlements

Her title carries more duty than grandeur. She still inspects scales herself and is often found with flour on one sleeve.

## Manner

Mara is practical, attentive, and slow to accept a comforting answer merely because everyone wants it to be true. She asks visitors what they know, what they inferred, and what they intend to do next.

She protects Skaggerty's independence without pretending the village can solve every danger alone. A person who brings evidence and a workable plan earns her support quickly; a person who brings panic gets a chair, water, and several exact questions.
