[[Simoon|Simoon]] owns [[../Locations/The_Stout_Fool|The Stout Fool]], the only true tavern and inn in [[../Skaggerty|Skaggerty]].

He is a diminutive human with a brash voice, sharp intelligence, and a dark sense of humor that works better on travelers than it does on everyone who has heard the same jokes for twenty years.

## The Stout Fool

Simoon lives above the inn in one of its few small rooms and is rarely far from the common room.

Because nearly every outsider in Skaggerty eventually passes through his door, he knows:

- Which caravans arrived
- Which road people used
- Who is hiring labor
- Who left town in a hurry
- Which local dispute is about to become public

## Personality

Simoon enjoys intelligent company and will happily argue with someone for an hour if business is slow.

He has little patience for customers who mistake a small town for an easy place to cheat people.
