[[The_Crossroads_Weighhouse|The Crossroads Weighhouse]] is Skaggerty's public scale house, records room, council chamber, and emergency granary office.

The front bay holds a wagon scale and inspection floor. A stone-walled rear room contains the village charter, road assessments, harvest tallies, and sealed keys to communal stores. The upper chamber is barely large enough for the council table and whatever residents insist on attending.

[[../NPCs/Mara_Sedge|Mara Sedge]] works here as Council Speaker and grain assessor. Notices posted beneath the awning cover road damage, livestock claims, militia watches, prices, missing goods, and meetings.
