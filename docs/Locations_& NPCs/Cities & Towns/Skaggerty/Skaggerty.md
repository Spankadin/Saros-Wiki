[Skaggerty](Skaggerty.md) is an agricultural market town of roughly twelve hundred residents near Hex 62.99, standing where routes toward [Goslow](../Goslow/Goslow.md), [Kylor](../Kylor/Kylor.md), and the northern road country separate.

Its importance comes from both position and the broad belt of fortified farms, villages, mills, and seasonal holdings that use its scales and storehouses.

## Character

Skaggerty is small enough that unfamiliar travelers are noticed. Grain carts, small herds, road dust, and the bells of [Temple of Austerus](./Locations/Temple_of_Austerus.md) are more common than luxury caravans or military columns.

The settlement is surrounded by fields worked close enough to town that farmers can return behind the palisade before dark.

## Trade and Economy

Skaggerty primarily gathers, mills, stores, weighs, and exports grain and basic agricultural goods produced across its wider farm belt. The town's population does not represent every farmer whose crop is sold under the Skaggerty name.

Inns, mills, granaries, teamsters, wagon services, and feed merchants benefit from the crossroads, but the town remains far less specialized than [Goslow](../Goslow/Goslow.md) or [Port Quartz](../Port%20Quartz/Port_Quartz.md).

## Travel and Connections

[Goslow](../Goslow/Goslow.md) lies roughly **40-55 road miles** away: 2-3 days walking, 3-4 days by loaded grain wagon, about 2 days mounted, or roughly 1 day for a fast courier.

Cargo intended for [Port Quartz](../Port%20Quartz/Port_Quartz.md) normally travels first to Goslow and is transferred to a ship. There is no ordinary safe commercial land route across the great rivers.

The northern road continues toward [Dun Karag](../Dun%20Karag/Dun_Karag.md) and [The Masys Mountains](../../Landmarks/Mountains/The_Masys_Mountains.md), while local western routes connect toward [Kylor](../Kylor/Kylor.md). Exact mileages for those legs remain unsettled.

See [Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population](../../../History_&%20Lore/Trade%20&%20Economy/Saros_-_Distances,_Travel,_Trade,_and_Population.md) for the canonical route and population model.

## Security

A modest palisade, local militia, and the occasional Legion patrol provide ordinary protection. The real defense is that Skaggerty is compact and its people know the surrounding land intimately.
