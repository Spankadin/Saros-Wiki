[[Harlan_Vey|Harlan Vey]] is Harbor Warden of [[../Ari|Ari]].

He comes from a fishing family but now spends most of his time mediating between fishers, grain factors, coastal captains, and prospectors moving toward [[../../../Landmarks/Mountains/Vy'Tungavor|Vy'Tungavor]].

Harlan has little patience for mining schemes that expect Ari to supply food and guards on credit.

## Manner

Harlan is plain-spoken and most comfortable on the working waterfront. He gives miners and captains the same treatment: pay what is owed, do not endanger the town, and do not expect Ari to finance a risky plan just because it sounds profitable.
