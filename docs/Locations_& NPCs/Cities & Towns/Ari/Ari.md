[Ari](Ari.md) is an eastern coastal town supported by fishing, nearby grain production, and small mining outposts working the lower country around [Vy'Tungavor](../../Landmarks/Mountains/Vy'Tungavor.md).

Its position makes it both a food producer and a staging point for prospectors moving toward the mountains.

## Character

Ari feels divided between sea and mountain. Fish markets and grain warehouses line the low ground while miners, prospectors, and pack trains gather farther inland.

## Government

A charter council led by [Harlan Vey](./NPCs/Harlan_Vey.md), the current **Harbor Warden**, balances fishing interests, farmers, merchants, and the owners of the smaller mining claims.

## Trade and Economy

- Fishing
- Grain
- Coastal shipping
- Ore from small Vy'Tungavor outposts
- Mining supplies
- Food and equipment for prospectors

## Travel and Connections

Heavy trade with [Rore](../Rore/Rore.md) normally follows the coast. A coastal trader can make the trip in about a day in favorable weather, while the overland journey takes roughly 4-5 days walking, 5-7 days by heavy wagon, or about 3 days mounted.

Inland pack routes lead toward the smaller claims and hatchling country of [Vy'Tungavor](../../Landmarks/Mountains/Vy'Tungavor.md), but no single dependable mileage describes those mountain journeys.

See [Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population](../../../History_&%20Lore/Trade%20&%20Economy/Saros_-_Distances,_Travel,_Trade,_and_Population.md) for the canonical route model.

## Security

Ari is fortified enough to resist raiders but cannot protect every mining camp in the mountains. Patrols concentrate on the road and harbor while outlying claims rely on hired guards.
