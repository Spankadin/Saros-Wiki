[[Captain_Lysa_Dorn|Captain Lysa Dorn]] is the March Warden of [[../Ani|Ani]], coordinating the town militia, coastal watch, and defensive preparations facing [[../../../Landmarks/The_Urmong_Barrows|The Urmong Barrows]].

She is not easily impressed by adventurers heading toward the Barrows. Ani sees a steady stream of confident people leave through [[../Locations/Barrowgate|Barrowgate]], and far fewer return with the same confidence.

Lysa works closely with the [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]] but considers Ani's local watch traditions indispensable.

## Manner

Lysa is not humorless, but most strangers meet her while discussing the Barrows, missing people, or defensive readiness. She has learned to ask adventurers about retreat plans before admiring their equipment.
