[[Ani_Watchhouse|Ani Watchhouse]] is the headquarters of the town watch and militia under [[../NPCs/Captain_Lysa_Dorn|Captain Lysa Dorn]]. Signal gear, reserve weapons, and Barrowgate duty rosters are kept here.

## Security

The structure is part of the settlement's ordinary defensive routine, not merely a wartime monument. Watch rotations, inspections, signal procedures, and emergency stores are maintained even during quiet seasons.

## Daily Routine

The Watchhouse keeps more than weapons. Patrol logs, signal codes, missing-person reports, and notes from travelers returning from [[../../../Landmarks/The_Urmong_Barrows|The Urmong Barrows]] are stored here.

[[../NPCs/Captain_Lysa_Dorn|Captain Lysa Dorn]] expects every watch officer to know the current condition of [[./Barrowgate|Barrowgate]] and the inland warning posts before taking duty.
