[[Breelyn|Breelyn]] is the navigator of [[../Locations/The_Storm_Runner|The Storm Runner]] and has been [[./Mirken_Stanton|Mirken Stanton]]'s closest friend since childhood.

Where Mirken is loud, confrontational, and instinctive, Breelyn is the person who can look at stars, current, wind, shoreline, and weather and turn all of it into a route. Mirken openly considers her the finest navigator he has ever known and would risk almost anything for her.

## Personality

Breelyn is brilliant at sea and spectacularly unreliable in port.

She is a colossal drunkard with a talent for disappearing into taverns, gaming halls, and back rooms for days at a time. When the Storm Runner is due to sail, Mirken and his crew sometimes have to search half the harbor district to find her.

The crew treats this less like an emergency than an annoying maritime tradition.

## Skill

Breelyn's knowledge of the waters and stars of [[../../../../History_& Lore/A_Brief_Saros_History|Saros]] is exceptional. She can judge currents, identify unfamiliar coastlines, and navigate by celestial patterns that most captains would not trust themselves to read.

Her drinking has damaged her reputation ashore far more than it has diminished the respect sailors have for her.

## Relationships

[[./Mirken_Stanton|Mirken Stanton]] is not merely her captain. The two have known one another since childhood, and their loyalty to each other predates the Storm Runner itself.
