[[Korth|Korth]] is the head priest at [[../Locations/Temple_of_Torm|Temple of Torm]] in [[../Goslow|Goslow]].

He is a very old human paladin with enormous white eyebrows that nearly hide his eyes and a frustrating tendency to fall asleep in the middle of quiet conversations.

## Past

Older Goslow residents remember a very different Korth.

Local stories credit him with rallying defenders during a major orc raid and holding the town together when panic might otherwise have broken its defenses.

Whether every heroic detail is accurate, nobody disputes that he fought.

## Present

Age has made Korth slow and frequently narcoleptic, but it has not made him foolish.

When awake, he is patient, difficult to shock, and unusually good at separating a genuine moral problem from an adventurer's attempt to have a priest approve something reckless.

He works closely with [[./Ostalda|Ostalda]], the temple's resident sage.
