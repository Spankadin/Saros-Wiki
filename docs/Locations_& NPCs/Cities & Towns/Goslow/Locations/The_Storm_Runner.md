[[The_Storm_Runner|The Storm Runner]] is a small troop transport captained by [[../NPCs/Mirken_Stanton|Mirken Stanton]] and navigated by [[../NPCs/Breelyn|Breelyn]].

The vessel carries a working crew of roughly one hundred and has a reputation for surviving coastal and river voyages that more cautious captains decline.

## Crew

[[../NPCs/Mirken_Stanton|Mirken]] commands the ship with a mixture of confidence, aggression, and personal loyalty to his people.

[[../NPCs/Breelyn|Breelyn]], his childhood friend and navigator, is regarded by Mirken as the finest reader of the waters and stars he has ever known.

## Reputation

The Storm Runner is associated with hard sailing, dangerous routes, and a crew that does not particularly enjoy being ordered around by the [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|Aegis Legion]].

Mirken's open hostility toward [[../../Grymswatch/NPCs/Admiral_Thainan|Admiral Thainan]] is well known among people familiar with the ship.
