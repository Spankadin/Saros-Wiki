[[Mara_Venn|Mara Venn]] owns [[../Locations/The_Drowning_Mermaid|The Drowning Mermaid]], the oldest tavern in [[../Kylor|Kylor]].

Mara knows which logging crews are overdue, which captains are lying about storms, and which strangers arrived without a believable reason to be so far northwest.

She is hospitable without being trusting.

## Reputation

Mara is a reliable source of practical town gossip because crews talk freely in front of an innkeeper they have known for years. She rarely repeats a confidence without a reason, but she remembers everything.
