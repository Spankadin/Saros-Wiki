[[Blackpine_Yard|Blackpine Yard]] is the largest saw yard and log boom in [[../Kylor|Kylor]].

Timber floated down from forest camps is chained, graded, cut, and prepared for coastal shipment. [[../NPCs/Osk_Fen|Osk Fen]] works from the yard when he is not in the woods.

Much of the best timber eventually reaches [[../../Goslow/Goslow|Goslow]] for shipbuilding.

## Workday

The site is busiest when goods are arriving, being processed, or being prepared for transport. Laborers, haulers, inspectors, and specialist craftspeople all depend on the work staying organized, so strangers wandering through active areas are noticed quickly.
