[[Admiral_Thainan|Admiral Thainan]] is the senior naval commander based at [[../Grymswatch|Grymswatch]] and one of the most powerful officers in [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The Aegis Legion/The_Aegis_Legion|The Aegis Legion]].

## Role

Thainan directs naval patrols and military operations concerned with the southern coast, especially the threat posed by [[../../Karastos/Karastos|Karastos]] and pirate groups such as the [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/Pirates/Bloodmarked/Bloodmarked|Bloodmarked]].

His command controls ships, supplies, crews, and the movement of military cargo across a wide stretch of Saros.

## Personality

Thainan is disciplined, proud, and comfortable making hard decisions without seeking approval from the people affected by them.

Supporters describe him as decisive.

Enemies describe him as a man who treats civilian suffering as a number that can be justified after the fact.

## Relationships

[[./Festin_Dontel|Festin Dontel]] serves as his lieutenant and trusted second.

[[../../Goslow/NPCs/Mirken_Stanton|Mirken Stanton]] considers Thainan a sworn enemy, and that hostility is well known among sailors who know either man.

## Reputation

In Legion circles, Thainan is respected for keeping pirate pressure away from settled coasts.

Outside those circles, stories about harsh seizures, aggressive patrols, and operations conducted with little explanation have made his name controversial.
