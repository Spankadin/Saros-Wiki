[Grymswatch](Grymswatch.md) is a fortified military town and one of the principal strongholds of [The Aegis Legion](../../../Factions_&%20Clans/The%20Aegis%20Legion/The_Aegis_Legion.md). It stands watch over the southeastern approaches of Saros and exists primarily to contain the threat posed by [Karastos](../Karastos/Karastos.md) and the pirate factions operating from that coast.

Almost everything in Grymswatch serves the garrison.

## Character

The town feels more like a fortress with civilian streets than a settlement that later acquired walls. Training yards, warehouses, barracks, signal stations, and repair sheds dominate the skyline.

Civilians are accustomed to inspections, drills, ration stores, and military schedules.

## Government

Grymswatch is governed under a military charter. [Admiral Thainan](./NPCs/Admiral_Thainan.md) commands the naval establishment and holds enormous practical authority, while [Festin Dontel](./NPCs/Festin_Dontel.md) serves as his lieutenant and second in command.

The arrangement is tolerated because the town's strategic purpose is defense rather than commerce.

## Trade and Economy

Grymswatch survives on:

- Naval supply contracts
- Ship repair
- Fishing
- Salted provisions
- Weapons and armor maintenance
- Timber and rope purchased from other settlements
- Crown expenditure

Without the Legion, the settlement would be dramatically smaller.

## Travel and Connections

[Karastos](../Karastos/Karastos.md) lies about a day away by coastal vessel. The difficult land journey takes roughly 3 days where usable, 3-4 days by wagon, or about 2 days mounted.

This short sea connection makes naval readiness more important than the land road and places [Admiral Thainan](./NPCs/Admiral_Thainan.md) under constant pressure to watch the pirate coast. See [Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population](../../../History_&%20Lore/Trade%20&%20Economy/Saros_-_Distances,_Travel,_Trade,_and_Population.md) for the canonical route model.

## Security

[Mira's Wall](./Locations/Mira's_Wall.md) is the great central fortress and training ground. Coastal patrols, watch towers, naval vessels, and constant readiness against [Bloodmarked](../../../Factions_&%20Clans/Pirates/Bloodmarked/Bloodmarked.md) raiders define life here.
