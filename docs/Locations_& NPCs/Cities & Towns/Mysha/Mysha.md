[Mysha](Mysha.md) is a southern logging town set where river, forest, and open land meet. Halflings are especially common in the settlement, and many local families maintain stories connecting their ancestors to [Lake Runa](../../Landmarks/Waters/Lake_Runa.md).

## Character

Mysha is low-built, communal, and deeply tied to the forest. Sawmills and river booms sit beside gardens, workshops, and stone yards.

## Government

A small **Woodward Council** manages cutting rights, river use, fire prevention, and disputes between logging crews. [Pellin Root](./NPCs/Pellin_Root.md) currently acts as Speaker.

## Trade and Economy

Mysha depends primarily on logging, but also produces:

- Carved goods
- Charcoal
- River timber
- Small amounts of quarried stone
- Tools and practical crafts
- Food from nearby farms and gardens
- [Smoke Leaf](../../../History_&%20Lore/Trade%20&%20Economy/Smoke_Leaf.md), including the halfling-grown varieties considered the finest in Saros

Much of its timber moves by river before joining southern trade routes.

## Travel and Connections

[Isoltide](../Isoltide/Isoltide.md) lies roughly **65-80 road miles** away: 3-4 days walking, 4-6 by wagon, or 2-3 days mounted. Water travel can be faster where the connected channels are navigable.

[Soryn's Crossing](../Soryn's%20Crossing/Soryn's_Crossing.md) lies roughly **45-55 miles** away: 2-3 days walking, 3-4 by wagon, about 2 days mounted, or 1-2 days for river freight when conditions cooperate.

See [Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population](../../../History_&%20Lore/Trade%20&%20Economy/Saros_-_Distances,_Travel,_Trade,_and_Population.md) for the canonical route model.

## Security

The town relies on local wardens, organized work crews, and a militia accustomed to forest emergencies. Fire is treated as seriously as monsters.
