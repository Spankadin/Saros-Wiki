[[Runa_House|Runa House]] is a communal hall in [[../Mysha|Mysha]] associated with local halfling families and their traditions concerning [[../../../Landmarks/Waters/Lake_Runa|Lake Runa]]. [[../NPCs/Pellin_Root|Pellin Root]] often conducts council business here.

## Public Use

Most residents encounter the building through ordinary civic business rather than ceremony. Notices, disputes, permits, records, and requests for local officials all pass through it, making the place a useful measure of what the settlement currently considers important.

## Community Role

Runa House hosts meals, family meetings, seasonal observances, and discussions about forest use.

Objects and stories associated with [[../../../Landmarks/Waters/Lake_Runa|Lake Runa]] are kept in several rooms, though residents do not treat the building as a museum for curious strangers.
