[[The_Quiet_Cup|The Quiet Cup]] is the only inn in [[../Brynk|Brynk]] routinely permitted to host outsiders. Its rules are posted prominently, and the proprietor expects them to be read rather than debated.

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.

## House Rules

Conversation is welcome; public disruption is not.

The innkeeper keeps a copy of Brynk's visitor customs behind the bar and will explain them once without complaint. A second explanation is usually shorter.

The Quiet Cup therefore becomes the place where outsiders quietly ask what a local rule actually means before testing it in public.
