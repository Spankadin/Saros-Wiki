[[Westlake_Ferry_Hall|Westlake Ferry Hall]] combines ferry offices, passenger records, and civic meeting rooms on the waterfront of [[../Albyrt|Albyrt]]. [[../NPCs/Mara_Avelin|Mara Avelin]] keeps her public office here. The hall's bell tower signals dangerous lake weather and temporary ferry closures.

## Public Use

Most residents encounter the building through ordinary civic business rather than ceremony. Notices, disputes, permits, records, and requests for local officials all pass through it, making the place a useful measure of what the settlement currently considers important.
