[[The_Reed_Crown|The Reed Crown]] is a large lakeside inn in [[../Albyrt|Albyrt]] favored by ferry crews and merchants. Its upper galleries look across the water toward [[../../Isoltide/Isoltide|Isoltide]], and arguments about which twin city has the better view are encouraged because they sell drinks.

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.
