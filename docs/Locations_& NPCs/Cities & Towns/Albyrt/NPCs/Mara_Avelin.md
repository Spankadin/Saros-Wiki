[[Mara_Avelin|Mara Avelin]] is the current Lake Reeve of [[../Albyrt|Albyrt]], responsible for balancing ferry interests, grain merchants, shoreline households, and the constant civic comparison with [[../../Isoltide/Isoltide|Isoltide]].

Mara is measured and patient in public, with the habit of asking for cargo numbers before entertaining grand political arguments. She came from a ferry-owning family and still knows the lake well enough to embarrass captains who exaggerate the weather.

She considers cooperation with Isoltide essential but dislikes any agreement that makes Albyrt appear secondary.

## Reputation

Mara is respected for treating lake traffic as infrastructure rather than ceremony. When weather turns bad, she is more likely to be found near the ferry office than at a formal council table.
