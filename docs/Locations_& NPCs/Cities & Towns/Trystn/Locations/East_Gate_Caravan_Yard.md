[[East_Gate_Caravan_Yard|East Gate Caravan Yard]] is the largest secure wagon yard in [[../Trystn|Trystn]].

[[../NPCs/Torv_Hallick|Torv Hallick]] bases many of his caravans here. Freight bound for [[../../Rore/Rore|Rore]] often waits beside northern cargo from [[../../Lilya/Lilya|Lilya]], making the yard a useful place to observe how goods actually move through the region.

## Workday

The site is busiest when goods are arriving, being processed, or being prepared for transport. Laborers, haulers, inspectors, and specialist craftspeople all depend on the work staying organized, so strangers wandering through active areas are noticed quickly.
