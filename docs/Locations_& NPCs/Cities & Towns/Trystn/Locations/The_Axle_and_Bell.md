[[The_Axle_and_Bell|The Axle and Bell]] is a famous caravan inn in [[../Trystn|Trystn]] operated by [[../NPCs/Jessa_Marr|Jessa Marr]].

Its courtyard is large enough to lock down several wagons overnight, and its common room is designed around people who expect to leave before sunrise.

The inn is a reliable place to hire guards, drovers, and guides for the road toward [[../../Lilya/Lilya|Lilya]].

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.
