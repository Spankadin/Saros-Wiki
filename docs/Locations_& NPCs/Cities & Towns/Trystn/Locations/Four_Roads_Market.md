[[Four_Roads_Market|Four Roads Market]] fills the center of [[../Trystn|Trystn]] with wagons, livestock, tents, grain, tools, and travelers.

Merchants from [[../../Port Quartz/Port_Quartz|Port Quartz]], [[../../Lilya/Lilya|Lilya]], and [[../../Rore/Rore|Rore]] all use the market, making it one of the best places in Saros to hear conflicting versions of the same news.

## Everyday Use

Trade here follows the rhythms of arrivals and departures rather than a fixed modern schedule. Prices can change sharply when a caravan, fishing fleet, or shipment is late, and local information often moves between stalls faster than it reaches any official notice board.
