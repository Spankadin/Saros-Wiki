[[Jessa_Marr|Jessa Marr]] owns [[../Locations/The_Axle_and_Bell|The Axle and Bell]], one of the busiest caravan inns in [[../Trystn|Trystn]].

She keeps a slate of arrivals and departures near the bar and can often tell who entered town, who left, and who quietly changed wagons in between.

Jessa dislikes violence inside the inn because broken furniture slows business.

## Manner

Jessa is cheerful while business is orderly and frighteningly efficient when it is not. She remembers caravan captains, unpaid tabs, broken gates, and the exact stable a traveler used months earlier.
