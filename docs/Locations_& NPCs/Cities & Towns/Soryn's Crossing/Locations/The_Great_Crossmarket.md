[[The_Great_Crossmarket|The Great Crossmarket]] is the wholesale heart of [[../Soryn's_Crossing|Soryn's Crossing]], where bulk goods change wagons, owners, and destinations.

## Everyday Use

Trade here follows the rhythms of arrivals and departures rather than a fixed modern schedule. Prices can change sharply when a caravan, fishing fleet, or shipment is late, and local information often moves between stalls faster than it reaches any official notice board.

## Market Rhythm

Different portions of the Crossmarket specialize by day and season: livestock, grain, timber, tools, imported luxuries, or wagon lots.

Merchants use painted poles and hanging signs so caravan crews can locate the correct section even when the square is packed.
