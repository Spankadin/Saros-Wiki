[[Three_Rivers_Yard|Three Rivers Yard]] is a secured freight compound in [[../Soryn's_Crossing|Soryn's Crossing]] with guarded storage, wagon repair, animal pens, and space for caravans waiting on road conditions.

## Workday

The site is busiest when goods are arriving, being processed, or being prepared for transport. Laborers, haulers, inspectors, and specialist craftspeople all depend on the work staying organized, so strangers wandering through active areas are noticed quickly.

## Security

Loaded wagons are assigned numbered spaces and sealed storage can be rented by the night.

The yard's guards care less about the moral character of cargo than whether fees are paid and the declared owner can prove a claim if another merchant challenges it.
