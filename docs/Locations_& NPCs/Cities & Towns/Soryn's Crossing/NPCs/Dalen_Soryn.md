[[Dalen_Soryn|Dalen Soryn]] is First Warden of [[../Soryn's_Crossing|Soryn's Crossing]] and a distant descendant of the family associated with the settlement's modern name.

Dalen is a merchant-administrator more than a noble. He understands toll schedules, warehouse capacity, and caravan contracts better than ceremony.

He is usually willing to make a deal if the result keeps traffic moving through the Crossing.

## Reputation

Dalen's family name carries history, but he works hard to avoid governing as though ancestry were a qualification by itself. Merchants generally consider him predictable, which in a trade hub is almost a compliment.
