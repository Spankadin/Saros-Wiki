[[The_Dealhouse|The Dealhouse]] is the civic seat of [[../Rore|Rore]], where [[../../../../Factions_& Clans/The_House_Table|The House Table]] meets and the city's public court is held.

The ground floor contains petition desks, record rooms, public notices, and court chambers. The upper hall holds the long table at which ward representatives, lenders, brokers, and commercial houses negotiate city policy under the eye of [[../NPCs/Cassia_Rook|Cassia Rook]], the First Dealer.

The Dealhouse handles crimes, civic disputes, licenses, and public law. [[./Salt_Court|Salt Court]] remains separate: it is a private neutral venue for contracts, debts, wagers, and commercial arbitration.

No gaming tables are licensed inside the Dealhouse. Rore's government prefers to keep its metaphors from becoming evidence.
